Toll Alert Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

Toll Alert reminds you to pay UK road tolls and the London ULEZ charge after you drive through them. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: your location stays on your phone. The only exception is an optional diagnostics feature, described below, which you can switch off.

Location

With your permission, Toll Alert uses your device's location, including in the background, only to detect when you pass through a monitored toll crossing or enter the ULEZ. Location is processed on your device by the operating system's geofencing service. It is never sent to us or to anyone else, and the app does not keep a history of where you have been.

Data stored on your device

The app stores the following locally on your phone: the crossings it has detected (which crossing and when), whether you have marked each one as paid, your reminder times and settings, and a short diagnostics log of geofence events to help troubleshoot missed alerts. Deleting the app deletes all of this.

Diagnostics (optional)

To help us fix missed alerts, the app can send its diagnostics log to a private Google Sheet that we own. You can turn this on or off at any time in Settings → Diagnostics. When it is on, the app sends: log entries describing geofence events and the app's health (permissions, whether location services were on, whether monitoring was armed), with any coordinates rounded to roughly 1 km before they leave your phone; a name, only if you type one in; a random identifier created when the app was installed; and the app version and phone operating system. We use this only to diagnose problems with the app. We do not sell or share it, and we delete it when it is no longer needed for that purpose. Contact us at the address below to have yours deleted.

Notifications

Alerts and payment reminders are scheduled locally on your device. We do not use push notification servers.

What we don't do

Toll Alert has no accounts, no advertising and no third-party tracking. Apart from the optional diagnostics above, we do not collect any personal data, and we never sell or share it.

Payment links

When you tap "Open payment site", the toll operator's official website opens in your browser. Anything you enter there is governed by that operator's own privacy policy.

Children

Toll Alert is intended for drivers and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
