# Toll Alert — AI demo video storyboard

Rob's ask (2026-09-24): "an AI demo video showing a crossing, the man going
to bed, putting his phone on the bedside cabinet, lights out, then the phone
alert at 11:45 — he gets out of bed, pays, and goes to sleep."

The prompts below work in current text-to-video tools (Google Veo, OpenAI Sora,
Runway, Kling). Most make clips of 5 to 10 seconds, so generate each shot
separately and join them in any editor (CapCut, iMovie). Target length is
about 30 seconds, portrait 9:16 for social media and landscape 16:9 for the
website.

**Consistency tip:** describe the man the same way in every prompt, for
example: *"a man in his 50s, short grey hair, navy jumper"*. Where the tool
supports a reference image, use a frame from shot 1.

**Don't let the AI draw the phone screen.** Generated text on screens is
usually garbled. Film the phone with a plain or green screen, then lay a real
screen recording over it in the editor. Record the web mockup or the app
(Home → "Simulate crossing (demo)" produces the real alert).

**Voice-over (optional, about 20 words):**
"Crossed the Dartford bridge and forgot to pay? Toll Alert reminds you
before midnight and takes you straight to the official site. Avoid the toll
fines."

**Before publishing:**
- Don't show a real number plate. Use a made-up one, or blur it.
- If you show the GOV.UK page, keep it to a quick glimpse. Don't imply the
  app is endorsed by National Highways.
- Add a small line: *"Phone must be in the vehicle. Reminder tool only —
  you remain responsible for paying."*
